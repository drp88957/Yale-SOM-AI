# Seller Brief

## Company

**Vancord**  
Website: https://vancord.com/

## Why I chose this company

I chose Vancord because it is a focused B2B cybersecurity and information-technology services firm rather than a giant general-purpose vendor. Vancord can sell to many kinds of organizations, but its best prospects are companies that depend on technology, handle sensitive information, or need help meeting security and compliance requirements. Those prospects are likely to have public websites that an agent can research.

## Information to collect for finding appropriate sales targets

The agent should collect these fields from Vancord's website and other reliable public sources:

- **Services offered:** Identify the specific services Vancord provides, such as cybersecurity assessments, managed security, incident response, compliance support, or IT consulting. This is needed to match a prospect to a concrete offering rather than making a generic pitch.
- **Ideal customer profile:** Record the industries, organization sizes, geographic area, and technology environments Vancord appears to serve. This helps filter out businesses that are too small, too large, or outside its practical market.
- **Business problems solved:** Summarize the risks or operational problems Vancord addresses, such as lack of an internal security team, regulatory pressure, or exposure to cyberattacks. These problems become the basis for relevant outreach.
- **Proof and differentiators:** Capture certifications, partnerships, case studies, testimonials, awards, and distinctive expertise. These details help explain why Vancord is a credible choice for a particular target.
- **Industries with sensitive data or compliance needs:** Note whether Vancord emphasizes healthcare, financial services, government, education, manufacturing, or other sectors. Sensitive data and compliance obligations are useful signals of a real need.
- **Target company name, website, and location:** Keep a verifiable identity for each prospect and avoid confusing similarly named organizations.
- **Prospect industry and approximate size:** Estimate the sector, employee count, locations, and growth stage from public information. These signals help determine whether the prospect resembles Vancord's customer profile.
- **Technology and security signals:** Look for public evidence of cloud dependence, multiple offices, online customer portals, recent expansion, security hiring, compliance language, or a published security/privacy program. These are useful indicators of potential demand, but the agent should label them as observations rather than facts about an unseen internal environment.
- **Likely decision-maker role:** Identify a public contact or role such as a CIO, CTO, CISO, IT director, operations leader, or compliance officer when available. The agent should not guess private contact details.
- **Personalized reason to reach out:** Write one evidence-based reason the prospect may benefit from a Vancord service, tied to a public source and clearly separated from assumptions.
- **Source URLs and research date:** Save the pages used for each important claim so a human can review the research and keep the lead information current.
- **Fit score and disqualification notes:** Rate the match and explain both positive signals and missing information. This keeps the agent from recommending every company it finds.
