# Ideas for the Wiki

## A. How to Store It (pick one first)
1. **Markdown + Obsidian (recommended start)**: Plain files in this OneDrive folder. Obsidian gives free backlinks, a graph view of how books connect, tags, and search. Works offline, needs no code, and the files are yours forever.
2. **Web app (black & pink)**: A searchable site with book, concept, and story pages, plus a "Surprise me" button. It can be built on top of the same Markdown files later.
3. **Both**: Markdown is the source of truth, and the web app reads from it.

## B. Features That Create "Aha" Moments
- **Situation finder**: Type "my startup has no differentiation" and get Blue Ocean, Zero to One (monopoly vs competition), and Start with Why.
- **Ask My Library (AI)**: A chatbot that answers **only from my wiki pages** and cites the page/book. It never pulls from the open internet. It would use Portkey + `gpt-5.6-luna`.
- **Random story of the day**: One story card a day to keep the books fresh in memory.
- **Book debates**: Pages where authors disagree, like Gladwell vs Epstein on specialization, Carnegie (agree with people) vs Grant's Think Again (challenge them), Thiel (avoid competition) vs Blue Ocean (make competition irrelevant).
- **Cross-book threads**: One idea seen through many books.
  - *Incentives*: Freakonomics → Logic of Life → Economic Naturalist → Naked Economics
  - *Amazon*: The Everything Store → The Four
  - *Bottlenecks*: The Goal → Toyota Way → 80/20
  - *Information & power*: Sapiens → Nexus → Chaos Machine → No Place to Hide → Anxious Generation
  - *Indian business*: Maruti Story, I Too Had a Dream, It Happened in India, Rebooting India, Connect the Dots
  - *Geopolitics of resources*: Prisoners of Geography → Chip War → The World for Sale → Currency Wars
- **Quote bank**: Short, memorable lines per book.
- **"Use it" prompts**: Every story ends with a question to apply it to my own life or work.
- **Interview/meeting prep mode**: Pick a topic (leadership, failure, data) and get 3 stories I can tell out loud.

## C. Suggested Build Order
1. **Pilot (1 book)**: Build *Outliers* fully (book page + ~8 story cards + concepts) so I can check the format.
2. **Scale**: Do all 60 books in batches by category. That's roughly 5–10 stories per book, so about 400 stories.
3. **Cross-link**: Concepts, situations, debates, people & companies.
4. **Verify**: I review pages and add my own highlights and notes.
5. **App + AI chat**: A searchable black-and-pink site with "Ask My Library".
