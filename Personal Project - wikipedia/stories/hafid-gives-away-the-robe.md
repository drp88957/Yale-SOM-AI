# Hafid's Unsold Robe: The Failure That Proved He Was Ready

**One-line hook:** A young camel boy failed his first sales test, gave away the robe instead, and that is exactly why his master trusted him.

## The Story
Hafid is a poor camel boy who works for Pathros, a great merchant. Hafid wants to become a salesman so he can become rich and marry the woman he loves. Pathros tests him: he gives Hafid a **single fine robe** and sends him to the village of Bethlehem to sell it.

Hafid tries for days and fails. Then he finds a newborn baby in a cave near an inn, sleeping in the cold beside a stable. Moved by pity, Hafid **wraps the infant in the robe** he was supposed to sell. He returns to the caravan ashamed, believing he has failed. But Pathros had seen a bright star following the boy. He recognizes it as the **sign he had been waiting for**: the one who should receive his secret. The robe wasn't sold, but the gift showed Hafid's heart.

Pathros then gives Hafid a chest holding **ten scrolls** containing the principles of success, along with money to start his own business. Hafid uses them and becomes the greatest salesman in the world.

## The Lesson
**Character matters more than one sale.** What looks like failure in the short term can be the clearest proof of the qualities that lead to long-term success.

## When to Use This
- You missed a target and feel like a failure
- You're tempted to cut corners to close a deal
- You're judging a junior person on a single result
- You wonder whether kindness has a place in business

## Try This
- After a "failed" attempt, list what it revealed about your values and skills, not just the result.
- When judging someone new, look at how they behaved under pressure, not only at the outcome.
