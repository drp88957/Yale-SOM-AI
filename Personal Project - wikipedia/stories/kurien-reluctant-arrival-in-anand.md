# The Man Who Didn't Want to Be in Anand

**One-line hook:** The "Milkman of India" arrived in Anand bored, unhappy and counting the days until he could leave.

## The Story
Verghese Kurien had trained as an engineer and studied abroad on a government scholarship. In return, he was **bound to serve** the government. He was posted to a government creamery in **Anand**, a small town in Gujarat. He did not want to be there, did not care much about dairying, and planned to leave as soon as his bond was done.

Nearby, a small farmers' cooperative, led by **Tribhuvandas Patel**, was struggling with old equipment. Kurien began helping them fix machines in his spare time. Tribhuvandas asked him to stay and help the cooperative. Kurien agreed to stay for a while. That "while" became the rest of his career. The cooperative grew into **Amul**, and Kurien helped bring the model to the whole country.

What changed was not the place but his **reason for being there**. Working for farmers who owned their own business gave the job meaning that the government post never had.

## The Lesson
A job you dislike can turn into your life's work when you **find a purpose and people worth serving** inside it. Meaning often comes from commitment, not before it.

## When to Use This
- You feel stuck in a role you didn't choose
- You're waiting out a job until "the real thing" starts
- Deciding whether to say yes to a small side request
- Feeling behind peers who seem to have better paths

## Try This
- Look for one person or group near you whose problem you could help solve this month.
- Say yes to a small, low-risk commitment and see whether it gives you energy.
