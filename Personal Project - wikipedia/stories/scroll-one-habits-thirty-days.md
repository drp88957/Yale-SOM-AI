# The First Scroll: Become a Slave to Good Habits

**One-line hook:** Before any sales technique, the first scroll teaches one thing: build habits by repeating the same words every day for thirty days.

## The Story
The ten scrolls Hafid receives from Pathros are meant to be read in a strict way. **The first scroll** explains the method. Its core claim is that the only difference between success and failure is **habits**. Bad habits can only be replaced by good ones, and you build a good habit by repetition.

So the scroll tells its reader to study **each scroll for thirty days** before moving on to the next, reading it **three times a day**: in the morning, again during the day, and finally aloud before sleep. Only after thirty days with one scroll do you move to the next. The goal is that the words become part of you, so you act on them without thinking.

Hafid follows this method faithfully. Over the years, the principles in the other scrolls (love, persistence, self-worth, living each day fully, mastering emotions, laughing at the world, multiplying your value, acting now, and praying for guidance) become his habits. His success comes not from one big moment but from steady, repeated practice.

## The Lesson
**Success is built by habits, and habits are built by repetition.** Focusing on one principle at a time for a month makes it stick.

## When to Use This
- You read lots of advice but don't change your behavior
- You're trying to build a new professional skill
- You feel behind and want a simple, steady plan
- You've tried to change many habits at once and failed

## Try This
- Choose one principle and reread a short written version of it three times a day for 30 days.
- Track each day you practice it on a simple calendar so you can see the streak.
