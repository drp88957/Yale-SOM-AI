# Arkad's Secret: A Part of All You Earn Is Yours to Keep

**One-line hook:** The richest man in Babylon started as a poor scribe with one simple rule.

## The Story
**Arkad** was known as the richest man in Babylon. His old friends, who had earned similar incomes, asked him how he got rich while they stayed poor. Arkad told them his story.

As a young **scribe** carving clay tablets, he met a wealthy moneylender, **Algamish**, and asked for the secret of wealth. Algamish told him: **"A part of all you earn is yours to keep."** Most people pay everyone else first: the baker, the tailor, the landlord. They never pay themselves. Algamish told Arkad to save **at least one-tenth** of everything he earned, no matter how little.

Arkad did it. Then he learned to make his savings **work for him**, so each coin earned more coins, and those earned more. Over time his wealth grew like a tree from a small seed. His friends' incomes had been similar; the difference was the **habit** of keeping a share and putting it to work.

## The Lesson
Wealth doesn't start with a big income. It starts with the **habit of paying yourself first** and letting savings compound.

## When to Use This
- You earn decently but never seem to save
- Planning your first salary or a raise
- Feeling behind friends financially
- Building any habit that compounds over time

## Try This
- Set up an automatic transfer of at least 10% of income to savings or investments on payday.
- Track your savings rate (not just your spending) every month.
