# Obama O's and Cap'n McCain's: The Cereal That Saved Airbnb

**One-line hook:** Broke and in debt, the Airbnb founders sold novelty cereal during the 2008 election, and it got them into Y Combinator.

## The Story
By 2008 the young Airbnb was barely alive. Few people were booking, investors kept passing, and the founders had piled up **credit card debt**. With the U.S. presidential election heating up, Brian Chesky and Joe Gebbia had an odd idea: sell **limited-edition cereal boxes** themed on the candidates, **"Obama O's"** and **"Cap'n McCain's."**

They designed the boxes themselves, bought cheap cereal, and assembled the boxes by hand. The stunt got press coverage, and they sold enough at a premium price to pay off a big chunk of their debt and keep going.

The cereal mattered most when they interviewed at **Y Combinator**. Paul Graham wasn't impressed by the Airbnb idea itself, but the cereal story showed him the founders were **resourceful and very hard to kill**. He accepted them, reportedly saying something to the effect that if they could get people to pay that much for cereal, they could probably get people to rent out their homes.

## The Lesson
**Investors and bosses often bet on the people more than the idea.** A creative way of surviving can be the best proof of your ability.

## When to Use This
- You're stuck and running out of time or money
- You need to show grit in an interview or pitch
- Your main project is stalled and you need a creative side move
- You're judging which team or person to back

## Try This
- In your next interview or pitch, tell one specific story of how you got out of a tight spot.
- When stuck, brainstorm ten weird ways to raise money, attention, or time, and try the least risky one.
