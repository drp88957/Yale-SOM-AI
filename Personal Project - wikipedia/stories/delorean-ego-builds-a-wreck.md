# John DeLorean: When Ego Runs the Company

**One-line hook:** A star executive left GM to build his own dream car company, and his ego drove it into the ground.

## The Story
**John DeLorean** was a rising star at **General Motors**, known for flashy success and a big personality. He left to start his own company, the **DeLorean Motor Company**, to build a stylish sports car under his own name.

Holiday describes how DeLorean's ego shaped the company. He focused on **image and lifestyle** rather than the hard work of building a sound business. He made decisions to serve his own ambitions, resisted people who disagreed, and mismanaged money. The car was late and had problems, and the company ran into deep financial trouble and collapsed, with DeLorean caught up in scandal.

Even afterward, Holiday notes, DeLorean tended to **blame others** rather than face his own role. That is the deeper lesson: ego not only causes failure, it **stops you from learning** from it.

## The Lesson
Ego can turn success into failure, and then hide the cause from you. **The leader who can't admit mistakes can't fix them.**

## When to Use This
- You or a leader is getting more attention than results
- A project is built around one person's image
- Things are going wrong and you're blaming outside factors
- Reviewing a failure honestly

## Try This
- After any failure, write down three things **you** did that contributed to it.
- Find one person who will tell you hard truths, and ask them for feedback regularly.
