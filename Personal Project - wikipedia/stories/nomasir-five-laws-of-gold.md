# Nomasir's Lesson: A Bag of Gold vs. a Tablet of Wisdom

**One-line hook:** A rich man's son lost a fortune, then won it back by following five simple rules.

## The Story
In "The Five Laws of Gold," the caravan leader **Kalabab** tells the story of **Nomasir**, son of the wealthy Arkad. Kalabab first asks his listeners which they would choose: a bag of gold or a tablet of wisdom? When Nomasir came of age, Arkad gave him both, a **bag of gold** and a **clay tablet with the five laws of gold**, and sent him out to prove himself.

Nomasir ignored the tablet. He lost the gold to gambling and to dishonest people with "sure" schemes. Broke and ashamed, he finally read the laws and followed them. Roughly, they say: gold comes to those who **save at least a tenth** of their earnings; it works for those who **put it to profitable use**; it stays with those who **invest under the advice of wise people**; it slips away from those who invest in **things they don't understand**; and it flees from those who chase **impossible earnings** or follow tricksters.

By living by the laws, Nomasir returned with far more gold than he'd been given, proving the **wisdom mattered more than the money**.

## The Lesson
Money without knowledge disappears; **knowledge without money can rebuild it**. Simple, boring rules beat clever schemes.

## When to Use This
- Receiving a windfall, bonus or inheritance
- Tempted by a "guaranteed" high return
- Recovering from a financial mistake
- Teaching someone young about money

## Try This
- Write your own "five laws" for money and put them where you'll see them.
- Before any investment, check it against the laws: understood? advised by experts? realistic return?
