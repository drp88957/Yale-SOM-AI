# "I Will Persist Until I Succeed": The Scroll for Rejection

**One-line hook:** The scroll every salesperson needs: treat each "no" as one step closer to "yes," and never let the day end on a failure.

## The Story
One of the ten scrolls Hafid reads is built around a single line: **"I will persist until I succeed."** It speaks directly to anyone who faces rejection. The scroll says the reader was not born for defeat. Every failure brings them closer to success, so each "no" should be welcomed as a step toward a "yes," and each setback as a lesson.

The scroll also gives practical rules. Don't measure your progress by how far you still have to go; just take **one more step**. Don't think about how many tries it will take. And when a day has been hard, **try one more time** before stopping, so the day ends with an effort rather than a defeat. Persistence here isn't wild optimism; it's the steady habit of continuing.

Other scrolls reinforce this, especially the one that repeats **"I will act now"**: procrastination is the enemy, so do the uncomfortable task immediately instead of waiting until you feel ready.

## The Lesson
**Most success comes from simply continuing after others quit.** Treat rejection as part of the process, not as a verdict on you.

## When to Use This
- You've had a run of rejections (sales, job applications, fundraising)
- You're tempted to quit a hard project halfway
- You're putting off a difficult call or task
- You feel you're not making progress fast enough

## Try This
- At the end of a tough day, make one more call, send one more email, or do one more rep before stopping.
- Track attempts, not just wins, so you can see effort adding up.
